# Impeccable design context — indmoney-watcher

## Design Context

### Users
A private Indian friend group (a handful of people who already share a WhatsApp group) checking
what US-stock bets each other are taking. Primary usage: evenings/night IST (US market hours),
on phones and laptops, in short check-ins — open, scan the feed, maybe banter elsewhere, close.
Read-only product: no orders, no money movement, sizes shown as % of portfolio only.

### Brand Personality
**Sharp, dense, trader-y.** A lean personal trading terminal — confident, fast, information-first.
Not cold or corporate: it's *our* terminal, for friends. Numbers are first-class citizens
(tabular figures, monospace where it earns it). No gamification, no confetti.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Dark theme by default** (evening use), light can come later.
- References: INDmoney/Groww familiarity (approachable fintech, clear instrument identity) ×
  Robinhood/Zerodha Kite restraint (minimal chrome, strong focus on the data, one accent used
  sparingly). Anti-reference: Bloomberg-terminal cosplay, neon-on-black "crypto dashboard" slop.
- Chat-adjacent feed density: an event row reads in under a second.
- **Chat messages are conversation-style (user decision, 2026-08-06):** built on shadcn's
  Message/Conversation components — avatars, message grouping, own-message distinction. Trade
  events remain dense system rows inline in the conversation; the terminal aesthetic applies to
  portfolio facts, not to speech.
- Semantic color discipline: entries/adds vs exits/trims get consistent, restrained treatment;
  accent color is rare and meaningful (60-30-10).

### Design Principles
1. **Scan speed over decoration** — every event row answers who/what/how much/when at a glance.
2. **Numbers are typography** — tabular/mono numerals, aligned columns, % of portfolio only.
3. **One accent, spent carefully** — semantic green/red for position direction; everything else
   stays in tinted neutrals (no pure black/white; neutrals tinted toward the brand hue).
4. **Dense but breathable** — tight rows, generous section rhythm; vary spacing for hierarchy,
   don't card-wrap everything.
5. **States are designed, not defaulted** — empty/loading/error each teach the product
   ("the watcher polls hourly during US market hours — next pass at 21:00").
